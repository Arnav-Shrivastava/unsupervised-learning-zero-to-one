# Unsupervised Learning: From Zero to Confident

A comprehensive, beginner-friendly journey into Unsupervised Machine Learning — covering Clustering (K-Means, DBSCAN, Hierarchical), Dimensionality Reduction (PCA, t-SNE, UMAP), and Anomaly Detection (Isolation Forest, Local Outlier Factor, Gaussian Z-Scores) from first principles to real-world customer segmentation and fraud detection.

## Project Overview
- **Zero to Confident**: Built for absolute beginners with intuitive analogies, hand-calculated examples, plain-English mathematics, and step-by-step NumPy implementations.
- **Hands-on Implementations**: K-Means from scratch, K-Means++, PCA via eigendecomposition from scratch, and scikit-learn comparisons.
- **Real-World Applications**: Customer segmentation and business persona generation using Mall Customer data, plus anomaly detection benchmarks.

## Notebook Sections
1. **Unsupervised Learning Foundations**: Labels vs. No-Labels, Distance Metrics, and Why Feature Scaling is Mandatory.
2. **K-Means from Scratch**: Initialization, Voronoi Assignment, Centroid Updates, K-Means++, and Sklearn validation.
3. **Choosing the Optimal K**: The Elbow Method (Inertia/WCSS), Silhouette Analysis, and when they disagree.
4. **When K-Means Fails & DBSCAN to the Rescue**: Non-spherical geometries, density-based clustering, and the k-distance graph.
5. **Hierarchical Clustering & Dendrograms**: Agglomerative clustering, linkage criteria, and cutting the tree.
6. **PCA from Scratch**: Covariance matrix, eigendecomposition, scree plots, and variance trade-offs.
7. **Non-Linear Dimensionality Reduction**: t-SNE, UMAP, Digits dataset visualization, and interpretation pitfalls.
8. **Anomaly Detection**: Gaussian Z-Score, Isolation Forest, and Local Outlier Factor evaluated side-by-side.
9. **Cap-Stone Project**: Real-world customer segmentation personas with actionable business marketing strategies.
10. **Assessments & Challenges**: 8 "Prove It" questions, 3 coding challenges, and 1 capstone extension idea.
